The Japan Rail Pass

One Pass, Unlimited Access

The most economical way to travel the Golden Route and access remote trailheads. Exclusive for international visitors, offering unlimited use of JR trains, including most Shinkansen and local buses.

Hands-Free Travel (Takkyubin)

Don't Carry, Forward

Hiking the Nakasendo or Kumano Kodo? Use Japan's 'Takkyubin' service. Send your heavy luggage from hotel to hotel overnight for a small fee, and enjoy the journey freely.
