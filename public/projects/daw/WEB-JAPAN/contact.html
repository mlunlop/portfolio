Plan Your Arrival

Visa Requirements

Most visitors can enter Japan for up to 90 days for tourism without a visa. Check the Ministry of Foreign Affairs website for specific country details.

International Airports

Narita (NRT) and Haneda (HND) serve Tokyo. Kansai International (KIX) serves Osaka and Kyoto regions.

JR Pass Information

The JR Pass offers unlimited travel on JR lines. Purchase your exchange order before arriving in Japan for the best rates.

Internet & Connectivity

Portable Pocket Wi-Fi or eSIMs are highly recommended. Free Wi-Fi is available but not ubiquitous outside major hubs.
